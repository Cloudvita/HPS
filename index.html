Your progress is saved in this browser.

    🎯 Interview Prep Essentials

    1. Aptitude & logic — most service-company drives start with an online test.
    2. DSA basics — arrays, strings, hashing, recursion, sorting. Solve 2 problems a day.
    3. Core CS — OOP, DBMS & SQL joins, OS processes/threads, HTTP & networking.
    4. Your projects — explain design choices, trade-offs and what you'd improve.
    5. HR round — prepare STAR stories: a challenge, a failure, a team conflict.
